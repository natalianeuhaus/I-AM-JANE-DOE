About the project

I AM JANE DOE brings together publicly documented cases, first-person accounts, and a developing portrait project to examine sexual violence and the systems that enable it.

It is a space for testimony, memory, and resistance. A name that can protect an identity can also become a collective voice.

Predatorpedia records cases and their aftermaths. In their words gathers statements with attribution and links to their sources. Portraits of Jane Doe is the next part of the project.
